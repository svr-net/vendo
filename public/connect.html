Connect your ad platforms

Vendo connects to each platform with OAuth: you sign in on the platform's own site and approve access, and Vendo never sees your password. Not ready for real money yet? Choose sandbox to practise with a simulated account.

🎓 What does "connecting" actually do?

  1. You click Connect. Vendo sends you to the platform with a one-time state code (protects against forged requests).
  2. You approve the permissions Vendo asks for (manage ads, read reports).
  3. The platform redirects back with an authorization code, and Vendo exchanges it server-side for an access token.
  4. The token is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and stored. You can disconnect at any time here, and revoke access in the platform's settings too.

You need an ad account with a payment method on each platform. Ads are billed by the platform directly. Vendo never handles your payments.
