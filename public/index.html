One brief → five platforms

You describe your goal, budget, audience and ad once. Vendo translates it into each platform's own structure:

PlatformWhat Vendo creates
● MetaCampaign → Ad set → Creative → Ad
● Google AdsBudget → Search campaign → Ad group → Keywords → Responsive ad
● TikTokVideo → Identity → Campaign → Ad group → Ad
● LinkedInCampaign group → Campaign → Sponsored post → Creative
● PinterestPin → Campaign → Ad group → Ad

Everything is created paused by default. You decide when money starts being spent.
